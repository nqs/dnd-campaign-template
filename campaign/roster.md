## Captain Halric Dane — Veyrhaven Civic Watch Captain

- **Role:** Lawful local contact and pressure valve; a Watch captain deciding whether to uphold containment orders or expose the Hall of Measures.
- **Affiliation:** Veyrhaven Civic Watch *(DM ADDITION)*.
- **Location:** Glassward cordon / Civic Watchhouse, Veyrhaven.
- **Status:** Alive; pre-play.
- **One-line summary:** A steady veteran officer who protects civilians first and will break procedure only when shown proof that procedure has become the danger.

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered human with a cropped iron-gray beard, a weathered blue rain cloak over practical armor, and a tarnished civic badge.

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Steadfast.
- *Public face:* Controlled, brief, and professionally skeptical.
- *Private self:* Angry at being asked to protect a lie.
- *Mannerism:* Checks the street behind him before giving an honest answer.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* Civilians alive; a defensible truth; his officers out of a political purge.
- *Fears:* Starting a panic or discovering that he has already enabled more deaths.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Dane has received sealed orders to restrict Glassward and amend death reports, but has not signed a false certificate. Mara’s originals can move him from cautious to openly allied. *(DM ADDITION)*

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* An obstacle with a badge.
- *What they want from the party:* Evidence strong enough to justify defying the Hall, and restraint around frightened officers.

> **Source notes**
> Invented for *The Ashen Grin* (Session 003, standalone). No combat stat block; see [the-ashen-grin-1-adventure.md](../sessions/session%203/the-ashen-grin-1-adventure.md).

## Elian Voss — Veyrhaven Civic Archivist

- **Role:** Compromised witness and route-holder to the Seventh Ward.
- **Affiliation:** Veyrhaven Central Archive / Hall of Measures *(DM ADDITION)*.
- **Location:** Central Archive, then the Hollow Court if pressured.
- **Status:** Alive; pre-play.
- **One-line summary:** A frightened archivist who helped hide the ward renewal and now needs protection to tell the whole truth.

**Appearance:** Gaunt human in charcoal clerk robes, one cracked lens, ink-stained fingers, brass archive keys, and a packed case he never quite leaves behind.

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Cornered.
- *Public face:* Exact, procedural, and evasive.
- *Private self:* Ashamed and trying to identify a survivable confession.
- *Mannerism:* Reorders keys whenever a question gets close to the truth.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* Containment restored without becoming the Hall’s sole scapegoat.
- *Fears:* Vorthax’s freedom, public disgrace, and being silenced by either side.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Voss holds the ward key and knows that three vents must be closed. If threatened without an exit, he alerts the Hollow Court and withholds that rule. *(DM ADDITION)*

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* A bureaucrat who knows more than he says.
- *What they want from the party:* A credible pledge of protection and a distinction between testimony and immunity.

> [!NOTE]
> **Source notes**
> Invented for *The Ashen Grin* (Session 003, standalone). No combat stat block; linked scenes in [the-ashen-grin-1-adventure.md](../sessions/session%203/the-ashen-grin-1-adventure.md).

## Mara Vey — Undertaker and Independent Investigator

- **Role:** Quest contact, evidence custodian, and moral witness for the victims.
- **Affiliation:** Vey House of Rest *(DM ADDITION)*.
- **Location:** Vey House of Rest, Veyrhaven.
- **Status:** Alive; pre-play.
- **One-line summary:** An undertaker with original death certificates who refuses to let a civic cover-up make the dead disappear a second time.

**Appearance:** Middle-aged human in a rain-black mourning coat, silver pin, leather gloves, close-cropped dark hair streaked with gray, carrying a hooded lantern and ledger case.

**Personality:**
- *In a word:* Principled.
- *Public face:* Quiet, precise, and relentlessly practical.
- *Private self:* Grieving for strangers because no one else will keep count.
- *Mannerism:* Says a victim’s full name before discussing a decision that affects them.

**Motivations:**
- *Wants:* Names restored, families protected, and evidence held where the Hall cannot erase it.
- *Fears:* Becoming another official who calls silence mercy.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DM:** Mara has copied the original certificates and hidden the copies separately. Destroying one ledger does not remove the evidence. *(DM ADDITION)*

**Relationship with the party:**
- *First impression:* A client who expects competence, not heroics.
- *What they want from the party:* Investigate the deaths and choose what truth Veyrhaven can live with.

> [!NOTE]
> **Source notes**
> Invented for *The Ashen Grin* (Session 003, standalone). No combat stat block.
