# Where We Left Off

![Bellwether at the Festival of Small Wonders](https://storage.image.mcp.nqs.io/images/aa1c68d8-7b1d-4fec-856e-102ad218ab4a.png)

This is a **standalone adventure**; no earlier events are assumed. You have arrived in **Bellwether**, a canal city celebrating its Festival of Small Wonders. Lantern Market is packed with merchants, performers, painted barges, and visitors. A string of coordinated pickpocket thefts has just disrupted the festivities, and the Canal Watch is looking for capable investigators. Some stolen property is valuable, while some is urgently personal.

**What now?**
- Speak with the victims and witnesses in Lantern Market.
- Reconstruct how several pickpockets worked together.
- Decide what a fair resolution looks like once the culprits are found.
