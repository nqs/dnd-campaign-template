### Triggers & Countdowns

- **Start:** Only roll initiative if Act II, Scene 2.2 meets an explicit encounter trigger. Fizzwick's first smoke puff allows one final sentence of negotiation before round 1.
- **Round 1:** Skritch opens the alley hatch; one performer begins carrying the property basket toward it.
- **Round 2:** If no stop condition has occurred, Maestro calls for parley while moving toward the hatch.
- **Round 3:** Any mobile goblin adjacent to an exit escapes rather than attacking.
- **Morale:** When any two goblins are incapacitated, all remaining goblins surrender or disengage and offer restitution.
- **Nonlethal default:** Goblins declare attacks nonlethal where the rules allow. Their tricks deal little or no damage and never target unconscious creatures.

### Tactics Summary

- **Maestro Nibs:** Directs allies, uses *Baton Feint*, then Dodges or Disengages; spends reactions to move endangered performers.
- **Skritch:** Uses *Tangle-Line* to restrain a pursuer, then Nimble Escape to reach an escape rope.
- **Goblin Performers:** One moves the property basket; others use flour, nets, Help, and Disengage. They do not focus fire.

### Loot / Aftermath

- All ledgered stolen property is present in the cellar unless a prior scene explicitly moved an item.
- Costumes and props have no meaningful resale value; taking them undermines a negotiated resolution.
- Captured goblins immediately propose full restitution in exchange for staying together pending Mina's judgment.

### Nimble Nibs Ringleader ⚗️

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), chaotic neutral · CR 2 (450 XP)*

**AC** 15 (studded costume) · **HP** 45 (10d6+10) · **Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 9 (−1) · **DEX** 18 (+4) · **CON** 12 (+1) · **INT** 13 (+1) · **WIS** 12 (+1) · **CHA** 16 (+3)

**Saves** Dex +6, Cha +5 · **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Deception +5, Performance +5, Sleight of Hand +6 · **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11 · **Languages** Common, Goblin · **CR** 2

**Traits**
- **Nimble Escape.** The ringleader can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action.
- **Nonlethal Rehearsal.** When the ringleader reduces a creature to 0 HP with a melee attack, the creature is stable and unconscious. The ringleader never attacks an unconscious target.
- **Role variation.** Maestro uses *Baton Feint* first; Skritch uses *Tangle-Line* first. Statistics are otherwise identical.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** The ringleader makes two Padded Baton attacks or one Padded Baton attack and uses Baton Feint.
- **Padded Baton.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6+4) bludgeoning.
- **Baton Feint (Maestro; Recharge 5–6).** One creature within 30 feet that can see Maestro must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or have disadvantage on its next attack before the end of its next turn. One goblin Maestro can see may move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Tangle-Line (Skritch; Recharge 5–6).** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one Large or smaller creature. On a hit, the target is restrained by marionette cord. A creature can use an action to make a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, freeing the target on a success. The cord has AC 10 and 5 HP.

**Reactions**
- **Places, Everyone!** When a goblin within 30 feet is hit, the ringleader lets that goblin move 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, provided it can hear the ringleader.

**Round-by-round tactics**
- **Round 1:** Maestro uses *Baton Feint* and moves toward center stage; Skritch uses *Tangle-Line* on the fastest pursuer and uses bonus-action movement toward an escape route.
- **Round 2:** Both ask for parley. If refused, use padded batons only against creatures blocking an exit.
- **Round 3+:** Disengage and escape. Use recharge abilities defensively. Reaction priority: move the most injured performer away from danger.

**On-defeat loot:** Costume purse with 3 gp and marked wooden audition tokens; stolen property is tracked separately.

### Goblin Performer ⚗️

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), chaotic neutral · CR 1/4 (50 XP)*

**AC** 14 (leather costume) · **HP** 13 (3d6+3) · **Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 8 (−1) · **DEX** 16 (+3) · **CON** 12 (+1) · **INT** 10 (+0) · **WIS** 10 (+0) · **CHA** 13 (+1)

**Skills** Acrobatics +5, Performance +3, Sleight of Hand +5 · **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 · **Languages** Common, Goblin · **CR** 1/4

**Traits**
- **Nimble Escape.** The performer can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action.
- **Nonlethal Rehearsal.** The performer uses harmless stage tools and never attacks an unconscious creature.

**Actions**
- **Padded Prop.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) bludgeoning.
- **Flour Puff (1/Day).** A 10-foot cube within 20 feet fills with flour until the start of the performer's next turn. Creatures in it are lightly obscured. A creature entering it or starting there must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution save or be unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn due to coughing.
- **Stage Net (1/Day).** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 5/15 ft., one Large or smaller target. On a hit, the target is restrained. Escape DC 10; net AC 10, 5 HP.

**Round-by-round tactics**
- **Round 1:** One carries the property basket; two use flour or nets; one uses Help for Skritch.
- **Round 2:** Disengage toward exits. A performer drops the basket rather than fight for it.
- **Round 3+:** Escape or surrender. No performer remains alone to cover a retreat.

**On-defeat loot:** Harmless stage props and 1d6 cp.
