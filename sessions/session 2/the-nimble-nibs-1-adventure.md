## Adventure Summary

During Bellwether's Festival of Small Wonders, a coordinated troupe of goblin pickpockets steals purses, keys, and personal belongings under cover of street theater. The thefts are real and disruptive: medicine, a wedding ring, and a treasured locket are among the missing property. The culprits, **the Nimble Nibs**, intended to return most personal objects after using them as props in a final caper. Their true goal is to recover a puppet trunk from the Festival Guild's secured prop annex and regain their impounded wagon, but their leader has buried that reasonable need beneath an unreasonable plan.

The party can solve the case through clues, social pressure, surveillance, magic, or direct pursuit. A successful ending must address both halves of the problem: victims need restitution, and the goblins need a lawful alternative to theft. Arrest, exile, cover-up, public audition, and negotiated community service are all supported outcomes.

## Running This Session

- **Tone:** Fast, colorful, and comic without treating victims' losses as harmless.
- **Pacing:** Run Act I, Scene 1.2 as a clue-selection montage. Use only two witnesses if time is tight. Run Act III, Scene 3.2 as a 5–10 minute debrief.
- **Failure:** Never gate progress behind one roll. Failed checks add complications, public alarm, or weaker leverage; visible evidence still points forward.
- **Violence:** Encounter 1 is optional. The Nimble Nibs use nonlethal attacks and flee or surrender quickly. They are offenders, not murderous villains.
- **Magic:** Divination and detection magic can accelerate the investigation. Reward useful spell expenditure with direct information rather than adding false wards.
- **Independent continuity:** Bellwether and its cast are DM additions. Nothing here depends on Talthryn, Caer Aldreth, or Session 1.

# Act I — Light Fingers at Lantern Market

## Act I, Scene 1.1 — The Pompous Victim

**Purpose / transition:** Establish the thefts, identify the stakes, and give the party three ways into the investigation: the coordinated routine, the Festival Guild key, or the theft route.

### Setting

- **Location:** Lantern Market, a broad plaza divided by canals and crossed by small arched bridges.
- **See:** Bright awnings, painted barges, street magicians, overturned baskets, and Osric standing on a crate before an unimpressed crowd.
- **Hear:** Vendors competing for customers, canal bells, laughter when Osric reenacts the theft, and mutually contradictory witness accounts.
- **Smell:** Citrus peel, fried dough, canal water, lamp oil, and cut flowers.
- **Sense:** Investigation proficiency or thieves' tools experience identifies a coordinated distraction-and-lift routine. *Detect Magic* finds fading illusion magic on a dropped card bearing a painted thimble emblem; the magic is a harmless sound effect, not a tracking aura.

### NPCs

#### Guildmaster Osric Pindle

- **Disposition going in:** Indignant and presumptuous; he treats any competent-looking adventurer as hired help.
- **Motivations:** Recover his purse and Guild key, conceal how thoroughly he was fooled, and turn the incident into support for stricter performer licensing.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If treated respectfully or promised discretion:** He offers **300 gp** for the purse, key, and token, with 100 gp payable immediately for the key alone.
  - **If his account is tested with DC 14 Insight or calm questioning:** He admits the purse held only 40 gp; the Festival Guild key and authorization token are the real concern.
  - **If publicly mocked or threatened:** He calls for a market-wide alarm. Witnesses become guarded, and the Nimble Nibs begin Act II, Scene 2.2 prepared to flee.
  - **Long-term:** Quiet restitution earns his grudging commercial favor. Public humiliation turns him into a comic bureaucratic rival.

#### Sergeant Mina Cobble

- **Disposition going in:** Amused by Osric, professionally concerned about the pattern.
- **Motivations:** Recover all property, prevent panic or collective blame, and resolve the matter without unnecessary injury.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If promised discretion:** She shares a map of five thefts; their sequence curves toward the Old Puppet Quarter.
  - **If asked about harm:** She names the medicine, ring, and locket, establishing that restitution matters.
  - **If asked for authority:** She gives the party a signed investigative chit, granting advantage on one social check with a civic clerk but not authority to arrest indiscriminately.
  - **If the party demands a raid immediately:** She refuses without a location or evidence and notes their impatience.

**Meaningful choices:** Protect Osric's dignity or leverage his embarrassment; prioritize the Guild key or vulnerable victims; work under Mina's oversight or independently.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act I, Scene 1.1 ended with Osric revealing the Guild key:** In Act II, Scene 2.1, Barnaby immediately connects it to the secured prop annex.
- **If Act I, Scene 1.1 ended with a public alarm:** Witnesses in Act I, Scene 1.2 require reassurance before volunteering details, and Niblet warns the troupe.
- **Otherwise:** The Nimble Nibs initially treat the investigation as an entertaining rival performance.
- **Transition:** Move to Act I, Scene 1.2 once the party has the theft map, thimble emblem, or victim list. If none was secured, Mina points out flour prints leading from the plaza.

## Act I, Scene 1.2 — Three Clues and a Custard Pie

**Purpose / transition:** Run a compact investigation montage. Let the party choose two of three clue stations; any two reveal the Old Puppet Quarter and either the impound yard or troupe emblem.

### Setting

- **Location:** The market theft route: Tessa's flower stall, Pip's pastry cart, and an arched bridge used by buskers.
- **See:** Chalk arrows at goblin eye level, a snapped marionette string, tiny boot prints in flour, and a custard pie tin beneath a bench.
- **Hear:** A hurdy-gurdy repeating the same phrase, vendors arguing over details, and occasional giggling from an upper window.
- **Smell:** Vanilla custard, crushed mint, wet rope, and cheap stage powder.
- **Sense:** DC 13 Investigation or Survival reconstructs several goblins moving in formation. *Detect Magic* identifies a minor illusion embedded in the repeated tune. Passive Perception 15+ notices a goblin lookout reflected in a polished kettle.

### NPCs

#### Tessa Thimblewick

- **Disposition going in:** Friendly and excited; cautious only if Act I, Scene 1.1 ended with a public alarm.
- **Motivations:** Recover her locket, tell an excellent story, and keep the smallest goblin from being hurt.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If treated kindly or promised restitution:** She explains that a small goblin removed medicine from the stolen-property basket and tried to return it.
  - **If pressed aggressively:** She identifies the thimble emblem but signals the lookout, causing Niblet to move the key evidence in Act II, Scene 2.1.
  - **If shown the emblem:** She recognizes it from an old Nimble Nibs playbill posted in the Old Puppet Quarter.

#### Pip Crumble

- **Disposition going in:** Defensive about the pastry-cart collision.
- **Motivations:** Avoid paying for ruined pies and conceal that the goblins purchased two pies before taking a third.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If paid 2 gp, helped with cleanup, or persuaded with DC 13 Charisma:** He says the goblins asked how to reach the municipal impound yard.
  - **If accused of conspiracy:** He loudly protests, increasing public alarm but accidentally reveals, “They only asked about their wagon.”
  - **If the flour trail is followed:** It reaches the Old Puppet Quarter and bears municipal-yard straw.

#### Goblin Lookout Niblet

- **Disposition going in:** Curious unless Act I, Scene 1.1 ended with a public alarm; then frightened.
- **Motivations:** Learn whether the party wants restitution, arrest, or a fight; warn the troupe if threatened.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If spotted and approached gently:** Niblet drops a municipal evidence tag from the impounded puppet wagon, then escapes.
  - **If chased:** Use a three-success-before-two-failures skill challenge, DC 13. Success catches Niblet long enough to hear “We only need our trunk”; failure still ends at the impound yard but alerts the troupe.
  - **If allowed to leave after the party speaks of helping victims:** Niblet reports that the party may be trustworthy.

**Meaningful choices:** Which witnesses to prioritize; whether to chase Niblet; whether to frame the investigation as punishment, restitution, or assistance.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act I, Scene 1.2 ended with Tessa warning Niblet or a failed chase:** In Act II, Scene 2.1, Niblet has removed the troupe playbill but left a fresh custard-and-sawdust trail.
- **If Act I, Scene 1.2 ended with Pip revealing the wagon:** Barnaby begins Act II, Scene 2.1 at Helpful rather than Neutral when shown Mina's chit.
- **Otherwise:** Chalk arrows and the evidence tag lead to the impound yard.
- **Transition:** Proceed to Act II, Scene 2.1 when the party identifies the wagon, impound yard, Old Puppet Quarter, or thimble emblem.

# Act II — The Method Behind the Mischief

## Act II, Scene 2.1 — The Impound Yard Ledger

**Purpose / transition:** Reveal the coherent target behind the theft: the Guild prop-annex key opens the secured annex where the troupe's registered puppet trunk was moved after its wagon was impounded. Establish a lawful solution.

### Setting

- **Location:** Bellwether Municipal Impound Yard beside the Festival Guild's secured prop annex.
- **See:** A brightly painted puppet wagon behind a municipal wheel clamp, citation notices across its doors, a stone annex with a brass Guild lock, and clerks chasing a goat wearing an evidence tag.
- **Hear:** Paper rustling, stamp thumps, wagon-wheel squeaks, the goat's bell, and muffled movement inside the wagon.
- **Smell:** Dust, old canvas, sealing wax, hay, and damp wood.
- **Sense:** DC 12 Investigation confirms the wheel clamp and annex lock use different mechanisms. If Osric disclosed the key in Act I, Scene 1.1, its crest matches the annex lock. DC 13 Insight reveals Barnaby is embarrassed by a misfiled permit, not hostile to goblins.

### NPCs

#### Clerk Barnaby Quill

- **Disposition going in:** Neutral and procedural; Helpful if Act I, Scene 1.2 ended with Pip's evidence and the party presents Mina's chit.
- **Motivations:** Preserve civic procedure, conceal that the wagon was impounded under the wrong subsection, collect a defensible fee, and make the goat someone else's problem.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If shown the theft map, evidence tag, or Osric's description:** He confirms the troupe's puppet trunk was moved into the Guild annex after the wagon was clamped.
  - **If the filing error is found with DC 14 Investigation or relevant legal background:** He reduces release costs from 100 gp to **25 gp**, provided the troupe earns provisional performance authorization.
  - **If the party helps catch the goat or reorganize the file:** He provides the standard audition form and promises not to summon the Watch for simple trespass.
  - **If intimidated, robbed, or bypassed:** He summons the Watch; Mina will arrive during Act III, Scene 3.1 unless the party resolves matters first.

#### Niblet

- **Disposition going in:** Hidden in the wagon; curious if the party emphasized restitution, frightened if earlier scenes ended with alarm or pursuit.
- **Motivations:** Protect the troupe, recover the puppet trunk, and judge whether the party can carry a lawful offer to Maestro Nibs.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If the party discusses a lawful release:** Niblet reveals themself and gives directions to the Crooked Curtain cellar.
  - **If the party threatens mass arrest:** Niblet flees with a rehearsal schedule, alerting the troupe and priming Encounter 1.
  - **If reassured and asked about the medicine:** Niblet admits Fizzwick set it aside and says no one was meant to lose anything permanently.

**Meaningful choices:** Expose Barnaby's error or offer him a face-saving correction; pay, negotiate, or reject the release terms; use Niblet as a bridge or pursue them as a suspect.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act II, Scene 2.1 ended with a legal release path:** Maestro Nibs begins Act II, Scene 2.2 hopeful; one successful DC 13 social check can secure a provisional agreement.
- **If Act II, Scene 2.1 ended with Niblet frightened or Barnaby robbed:** The troupe begins Act II, Scene 2.2 defensive; the first social check is DC 16, and Encounter 1 is primed.
- **If Act II, Scene 2.1 ended with Barnaby summoning the Watch:** Mina arrives in Act III, Scene 3.1 with the case still open.
- **Otherwise:** The party reaches the cellar with evidence but no ready legal solution.
- **Transition:** Move to Act II, Scene 2.2 when the party obtains the rehearsal location from Niblet, the schedule, or the playbill ledger.

## Act II, Scene 2.2 — Rehearsal Beneath the Crooked Curtain

**Purpose / transition:** Confront the Nimble Nibs, recover the property, and decide whether the finale begins with cooperation, capture, or pursuit.

### Setting

- **Location:** A disused cellar theater beneath the Crooked Curtain tavern.
- **See:** Painted flats, marionettes, crossing ropes, labeled baskets of stolen property, flour sacks, ladders, and goblins rehearsing pocket-lifting as choreography.
- **Hear:** Whispered counting, puppet clacks, a poorly tuned fiddle, and abrupt silence when the party is noticed.
- **Smell:** Greasepaint, candle wax, mushroom stew, sawdust, and old velvet.
- **Sense:** DC 12 Investigation shows valuables are catalogued rather than spent. DC 13 Insight reveals most troupe members are proud of their skill but uncomfortable with the thefts. *Locate Object* can identify any listed item unless it is inside the lead-lined stage safe; none initially are.

### NPCs

#### Maestro Nibs

- **Disposition going in:** Hopeful if Act II, Scene 2.1 ended with a legal path; defensive if Niblet was frightened; otherwise grandiose and curious.
- **Motivations:** Recover the wagon and puppet trunk, keep the troupe together, and prove goblin performers deserve a festival place.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If offered the 25 gp release path and an audition:** He returns all property and accepts supervised restitution after one DC 13 Persuasion, Performance, or legal-reasoning check.
  - **If confronted first with medicine, ring, or locket:** He is genuinely appalled, orders immediate return, and lowers any negotiation DC by 2.
  - **If offered escape without restitution:** He accepts, but Fizzwick and Niblet object; Mina later treats the party as complicit.
  - **If threatened with immediate violence or indiscriminate arrest:** He triggers the theater escape routine and Encounter 1.

#### Skritch “Quickpocket”

- **Disposition going in:** Competitive and protective.
- **Motivations:** Keep younger goblins free, demonstrate her craft, and avoid admitting she kept Tessa's locket because she admired it.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If challenged to a harmless skills exhibition:** She proposes marked tokens and bells for the festival act; this counts as evidence of a safe performance.
  - **If Tessa's kindness is mentioned:** She returns the locket and supports Maestro's surrender.
  - **If treated only as a criminal:** She pulls the escape rope, priming or beginning Encounter 1.

#### Fizzwick

- **Disposition going in:** Nervous and ready to confess.
- **Motivations:** Return dangerous and sentimental items, save the wagon, and perform his exploding-turnip finale.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If questioned separately:** He reveals every property location and both escape routes.
  - **If tempers rise:** Harmless stage smoke erupts, giving either side a chance to de-escalate before initiative.
  - **If encouraged:** He volunteers to convert all thief tools into obvious stage props.

**Meaningful choices:** Demand arrest, negotiate restitution, sponsor an audition, permit exile, or accept the troupe's proposed public skills test.

**Encounter:** **Encounter 1 — The Crooked Curtain Scramble** (File 2), optional. Trigger only if negotiation fails, a character initiates force, immediate indiscriminate arrest is attempted, or the party chooses a forceful pursuit. The troupe uses nonlethal tactics and stops when any listed de-escalation condition is met.

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act II, Scene 2.2 ended with agreement and full property recovery:** Act III, Scene 3.1 is a provisional audition; Amara begins Neutral and Mina begins Helpful.
- **If Act II, Scene 2.2 ended with the troupe escaping:** Act III, Scene 3.1 is a public pursuit folded into an unscheduled performance.
- **If Act II, Scene 2.2 ended with capture:** Act III, Scene 3.1 is a civic hearing at the festival stage.
- **If Act II, Scene 2.2 ended with escape offered without restitution:** Mina begins Hostile to that plan, and victims publicly present their claims.
- **Transition:** Proceed to Act III, Scene 3.1 once the party has an agreement, prisoners, or a trail to the festival stage.

# Act III — The Festival's Smallest Headliners

## Act III, Scene 3.1 — The Festival Audition

**Purpose / transition:** Resolve guilt, restitution, and the troupe's future through a public audition, pursuit, or hearing.

### Setting

- **Location:** Bellwether's Festival Stage beside the central canal.
- **See:** Colorful banners, a temporary stage, an expectant crowd, Osric's front-row chair, and the clamped puppet wagon across the canal.
- **Hear:** Festival bells, applause, officials debating permits, and goblins whispering about a missing finale puppet.
- **Smell:** Sugared almonds, river air, hot wax, fried onions, and stage smoke.
- **Sense:** DC 12 Insight shows the crowd wants permission to laugh rather than punishment. Performance proficiency or DC 13 social insight identifies Osric's public dignity as the main obstacle to compromise.

### NPCs

#### Adjudicator Amara Vale

- **Disposition going in:** Neutral if a legal path exists; skeptical if property remains missing or the troupe arrives as fugitives.
- **Motivations:** Deliver a good festival, avoid scandal, and preserve enough procedure to keep her office credible.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If all priority property is returned and a safe act is demonstrated:** She grants a provisional festival license.
  - **If thefts are concealed:** She may grant a license, but later discovery creates a fraud loose end.
  - **If the troupe arrives as fugitives:** She allows one three-minute performance before judgment if the party accepts responsibility for safety.

#### Guildmaster Osric Pindle

- **Disposition going in:** Helpful if his key and dignity were restored quietly; Hostile if mocked or if the key is missing.
- **Motivations:** Recover property, control the public story, and avoid being the festival's punchline.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If credited as the troupe's reforming sponsor:** He waives the 25 gp release charge and reframes the episode as his idea.
  - **If publicly exposed:** He demands arrest but loses crowd support; DC 13 Persuasion can redirect him toward restitution.
  - **If the key remains missing:** He blocks the license until it is produced.

#### Sergeant Mina Cobble

- **Disposition going in:** Helpful after full restitution; Neutral after capture; Hostile if the party enabled escape without restitution or used excessive force.
- **Motivations:** Secure restitution, impose proportionate consequences, and avoid filling cells with performers.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If all priority property is returned:** She accepts community service and supervised performance.
  - **If only wealthy victims are repaid:** She insists on medicine, ring, and locket first.
  - **If force caused injuries:** She records the injuries and reduces or withholds the civic reward.

**Meaningful choices:** Give Osric face-saving credit or expose him; advocate license, exile, or arrest; reveal or conceal the full theft record; put the party's reputation behind a public test.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act III, Scene 3.1 ended with a license:** The Nimble Nibs become legitimate performers and potential urban informants.
- **If Act III, Scene 3.1 ended with restitution but no license:** The troupe leaves Bellwether with its wagon under a no-performance order.
- **If Act III, Scene 3.1 ended with imprisonment:** Merchants approve; performers and poorer residents view the party less favorably.
- **If Act III, Scene 3.1 ended with a cover-up:** The troupe is free, but Mina opens a quiet inquiry if any property remains missing.
- **Transition:** Move to Act III, Scene 3.2 once Amara or Mina announces terms and the party accepts, rejects, or circumvents them.

## Act III, Scene 3.2 — Pockets Emptied, Accounts Settled

**Purpose / transition:** Spend 5–10 minutes resolving rewards, property, relationships, and loose ends. This scene contains no new obstacle.

### Setting

- **Location:** A canal-side café after the festival proceedings.
- **See:** Recovered possessions on numbered cloths, signed receipts, tired goblins sharing an enormous pastry, and the wagon departing, returning, or remaining clamped.
- **Hear:** Coins counted, victims thanking or complaining, sunset canal bells, and distant applause.
- **Smell:** Coffee, cinnamon, ink, damp stone, and cooling pastry.
- **Sense:** DC 12 Insight confirms Maestro intends to abandon theft if the troupe retains a livelihood. If humiliated and separated without an alternative, he instead plans to regroup elsewhere.

### NPCs

#### Maestro Nibs

- **Disposition going in:** Grateful, chastened, resentful, imprisoned, or absent according to Act III, Scene 3.1.
- **Motivations:** Keep the troupe together, settle obligations, and preserve some theatrical dignity.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If licensed:** He gives each PC an honorary brass thimble badge and offers future street intelligence.
  - **If exiled after restitution:** He promises to avoid Bellwether's pockets, carefully excluding other cities until pressed to adopt a broader code.
  - **If arrested:** He performs one final harmless coin vanish, then immediately returns the coin.

#### Sergeant Mina Cobble

- **Disposition going in:** Determined by the party's proportionality and restitution record.
- **Motivations:** Close the case accurately and prevent Osric from rewriting the entire event.
- **Possible outcomes and effects:**
  - **If all property is returned peacefully:** She pays a **200 gp civic reward** in addition to any payment Osric owes.
  - **If property remains missing:** She withholds 50 gp per priority item, up to the full civic reward.
  - **If the party caused unnecessary damage:** She deducts documented repair costs.

**Meaningful choices:** Accept Osric's preferred public story; forgive or collect minor losses; invite the troupe as contacts; require a code of conduct.

**Encounter:** None

**Conditional follow-through:**
- **If Act III, Scene 3.2 ended with all priority possessions returned:** The case closes without a serious victim left behind.
- **If Act III, Scene 3.2 ended with Osric repaid but vulnerable victims ignored:** The party receives Osric's money but loses Mina's respect and civic reward.
- **If Act III, Scene 3.2 ended with a licensed troupe:** The party gains the Nimble Nibs as a Bellwether information contact.
- **Otherwise:** The troupe may recur as comic rivals with an improved technique and an unresolved moral debt.
