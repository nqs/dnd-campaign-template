# Session 002 — The Nimble Nibs

*Tier 2 · Level 6 (5 independent PCs) · Bellwether (DM ADDITION)*

**Status:** ⏳ Not yet played — summary reflects *planned major beats, not a record of play*.

## Summary

This independent one-shot does not assume that Session 1 or *The Empty Throne* occurred. In the canal city of **Bellwether**, the party investigates a comic but consequential series of festival thefts committed by the goblin puppet troupe known as **the Nimble Nibs**. The planned beats are to reconstruct the troupe's coordinated pickpocket routine in Lantern Market, connect a stolen festival key to their impounded wagon and props, confront them beneath the Crooked Curtain, and decide whether justice means arrest, restitution, exile, or a legitimate public performance. One optional nonlethal encounter can occur if negotiations collapse; the session otherwise supports a fully investigative and social resolution.
