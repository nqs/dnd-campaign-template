## Pacing

- **Target:** 2–2.5 hours.
- **Act I, Scene 1.2:** Use two clue stations, not all three, unless players want a longer investigation.
- **Act II, Scene 2.2:** Skip Encounter 1 if a credible restitution offer is made.
- **Act III, Scene 3.2:** 5–10 minutes; no new obstacle.
- **Extended runtime:** 2.5–3 hours if every witness, public debate, and optional encounter runs fully.

## Encounter 1 — De-escalation

- **Math:** 1,100 raw XP; ×2 for six enemies = 2,200 adjusted XP; Easy threshold 1,500.
- **Stop immediately if:** any two goblins incapacitated; Maestro persuaded; priority property displayed; credible lawful release offered; exits blocked without violence.
- **Round 1:** Open alley hatch; move property basket.
- **Round 2:** Maestro calls for parley.
- **Round 3:** Goblins at exits escape.
- **No lethal intent:** Tricks, nets, flour, padded props; never attack unconscious creatures.

## Tone / Staging Beats

- Bells and repeated music turn the investigation into choreography.
- Osric's indignity is funny; stolen medicine and sentimental items are not.
- Barnaby's goat interrupts bureaucracy when pacing drags.
- Maestro describes scenes as acts; Skritch counts movements under her breath.
- Harmless smoke provides a pause before initiative, not an automatic fight.
- Let the crowd's laughter become approval only after restitution is addressed.
