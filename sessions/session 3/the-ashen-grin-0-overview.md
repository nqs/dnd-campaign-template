# Session 003 — The Ashen Grin

*Tier 3 · Five 10th-level characters · Veyrhaven (DM ADDITION)*

**Status:** ⏳ Not yet played — summary reflects *planned major beats, not a record of play*.

## Summary

This standalone dark-urban-horror session assumes no outcome from Sessions 1 or 2. In **Veyrhaven**, a rain-soaked canal city whose officials quietly erase inconvenient deaths, the party investigates a sequence of ritual murders marked by ash caught inside the victims’ smiles. The planned route runs from the flooded glass baths through a compromised civic archive and a ruined tribunal, before confronting **Vorthax, the Ashen Grin** beneath the sealed Seventh Ward. The party can expose the cover-up, break the ward-engine, bargain for delayed disaster, or let Veyrhaven keep its secret at a cost. All Veyrhaven people, places, factions, and stat blocks are **(DM ADDITION)**.
