## Ash Clock / Ward Engine

| Clock | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 — Dormant | Vorthax has no regeneration; attendants cannot return |
| 1 — First vent opens | 15-foot ash cloud: lightly obscured; Con save or no reactions |
| 2 — Reflections turn | Vorthax can teleport between reflective surfaces once/round |
| 3 — Names erased | One conscious creature in chamber: DC 16 Wis or frightened until end of next turn |
| 4 — Civic breach | Vorthax gains 20 temporary HP; storm spreads across Glassward; containment becomes a post-session crisis |

**Reduce clock:** action at a marked conduit, DC 16 Arcana/Religion/Thieves’ Tools; success closes one vent. *Dispel magic* at 5th level or higher closes one automatically. A creature carrying Voss’s ward key has advantage. Three closed vents reset the clock to 0.

## Tone / Staging Beats

- Rain gets quieter whenever the ash clock advances.
- No victim is grotesque: the horror is bureaucratic indifference and a wrong expression.
- Let archive dust mimic ash until the players test it.
- Vorthax never shouts; it speaks like a clerk correcting a form.
- Mirrors show a reflection one heartbeat late before they show anything impossible.
